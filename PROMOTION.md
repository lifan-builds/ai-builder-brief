# Staggered launch plan

Promotion starts only after CastForge is installable and AI Builder Brief passes its seven-run shadow gate.

1. **CastForge release:** demonstrate `init → run → validate` with the offline fixture.
2. **Nitan retrospective:** share the real MIME, zero-length RSS, provider-state, and failed-week lessons behind the framework.
3. **AI Builder Brief beta:** launch the feed with its architecture and one readable episode manifest.
4. **Show HN:** focus on source qualification, transparent artifacts, and fail-closed R2/RSS publication.
5. **Targeted technical posts:** tailor the same proof for Python, self-hosting, podcasting, and NotebookLM communities; follow each channel's self-promotion rules.
6. **Thirty-day report:** publish on-time rate, duration distribution, corrections, source mix, downloads, CastForge traffic, and external setup attempts.

## Draft angles

- “What 18 automated podcast episodes taught us about RSS contracts.”
- “Show HN: an open, source-transparent six-minute AI briefing.”
- “How an AI podcast fails closed when NotebookLM or object storage fails.”
- “From one opinionated forum show to a reusable Python framework.”

Do not post identical launch copy across communities or use automated promotional accounts.
