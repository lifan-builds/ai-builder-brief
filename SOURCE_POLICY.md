# Source policy

## Eligible factual sources

- Official announcements, model cards, release notes, repositories, and research papers are primary sources.
- Established reporting can corroborate a story. A cluster without a primary source requires two independent publications.
- Hacker News, Techmeme, GitHub activity, likes, downloads, and discussion volume are discovery/ranking signals. They cannot qualify a story alone.

## Content use

- Store titles, URLs, dates, attribution, and short feed/page summaries required to explain the event.
- Link to the original source in the manifest, show notes, and chapters.
- Do not republish full articles, bypass paywalls, or treat newsletter summaries as factual sources.
- Do not ingest Reddit content. Reddit's API and user-content terms require a separately approved use case.
- Do not quote community comments unless the source and author permission clearly allow it.

## Editorial rules

- Explain what happened, why a builder should care, and what remains uncertain.
- Never infer capability from a benchmark without stating the benchmark's scope.
- Distinguish announced availability from demonstrated or generally available capability.
- A correction to a fact, number, attribution, or capability description is material and must be logged.
