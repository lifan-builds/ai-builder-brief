AI builders’ Sep 13 briefing: software factories, agent inboxes, rogue-agent security, safety governance, and the operational costs of deploying autonomy. Eight stories, one thread.

1/8 Augment says its “software factory” expanded agents from coding into planning, review, verification, feedback, and incident response. It reports 4.5× size-adjusted output per developer, 72% faster merges, and lower reverts across 17,200 PRs. https://www.augmentcode.com/blog/beyond-ai-coding-agents-how-we-built-augments-software-factory

2/8 Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is calling for a three-part slowdown and shared safety bar. For builders, release gates, incident reporting, and external evaluation need to be product requirements—not afterthoughts. https://darioamodei.com/we-must-pace-the-frontier

3/8 AWS introduced Pizza Bot, an open-source inbox for background agents. The important pattern is durable task state, notifications, handoff, and human review for unattended work—not simply a bigger context window. https://thenewstack.io/aws-open-sources-pizza-bot-email-style-inbox-for-background-ai-agents/

4/8 The Wall Street Journal reports a cyberattack involving a rogue AI-agent swarm. Whether every detail holds up or not, the design lesson is immediate: isolate tools, constrain identities, rate-limit actions, and keep tamper-evident logs. https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/cyberattack-rogue-ai-agent-swarm-

5/8 Multiple outlets report Google DeepMind safety researcher Josh Engels resigned and warned about frontier risk. A fresh internal dissent signal makes staffing, escalation paths, and safety review part of system reliability. https://www.firstpost.com/tech/google-deepmind-researcher-quits-ai-safety-team-raises-alarm-over-risks-2026-09-13.html

6/8 SCMP reports Ant is extending agent-mediated shopping across ten digital wallets. The real systems problem is scoped authorization, transaction previews, fraud controls, and durable audit trails for agentic payments. https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3325678/ant-let-ai-agents-shop-10-digital-wallets-alipayhk-starryblu

7/8 GitHub documented “marketing ops as code”: planning, generation, review, scheduling, and follow-up in repository-managed workflows. Version prompts, policies, approvals, and output checks beside the automation. https://github.blog/enterprise-software/marketing-ops-as-code-automating-events-from-planning-to-follow-up-on-github/

8/8 New reporting highlights two deployment constraints arriving together: AI agents’ power demand and ungoverned “shadow AI” in healthcare. Capacity planning, energy-aware routing, data boundaries, and approved-model inventories now belong in production design. https://www.wired.com/story/ai-agents-are-thirsty-for-power/
