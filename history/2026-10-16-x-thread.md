https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open

https://huggingface.co/blog/reflection-beam

4/ NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 release includes Bolt, a 74B/9B-active model compressed toward a 24GB budget. The recipe combines expert pruning, ~2-bit weights, and speculative decoding. Reported speed is hardware-specific. E4B arrives Oct 23.
https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-24GB/

https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai/Step-5-Preview
