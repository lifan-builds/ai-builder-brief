AI Top 10 for Sept. 9: OpenAI claims a 10,000-agent system solved Navier–Stokes; Meta gives a consumer agent real app access; and fresh data shows coding agents becoming both governed team infrastructure and serious enterprise spend. The builder brief:

1/ OpenAI says ~10,000 agents spent 88 hours producing a Navier–Stokes proof, then Astra formalized it in Lean. The artifact is public; Clay has not validated it, and a priority/data dispute remains. https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/

2/ Meta launched Muse, a persistent cloud agent that can use email, calendars, payments, health and smart-home apps. Internal tests found useful travel work—and serious guardrail and reliability failures. https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-launches-ai-agent-that-can-access-other-apps-send-emails-make-payments-2026-09-08/

3/ DeepMind’s AlphaGenome Atlas publishes predictions for all ~9B possible single-letter DNA changes. It turns a costly model into queryable research infrastructure, but predictions still need experimental validation. https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-atlas-a-predictive-map-of-every-possible-dna-letter-change-in-the-human-genome/

4/ Cognition says Devin’s run-rate revenue jumped from $492M in May to almost $900M, alongside a $2B+ round. Company-reported, but one of the clearest time-bounded signals that coding agents are entering enterprise budgets. https://x.com/cognition/status/2097369798518681891

5/ Tencent’s TeamAI CLI is standardizing skills, rules, MCP, memory and review across Codex, Claude Code, Cursor and more. Fresh releases plus 1,083 GitHub stars today suggest teams want agent context managed like code. https://github.com/Tencent/teamai-cli

6/ Suno v6 was trained with Warner Music and BMG; participating artists can opt in, and labels/publishers get royalties. This is a real test of licensed models as a post-lawsuit template for generative media. https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/suno-releases-new-ai-music-models-partnership-with-warner-music-bmg-2026-09-09/

7/ GPT Images 2.5 is live in the API, ChatGPT and Codex, with sketch input, more precise edits and up to 50% lower latency, per OpenAI. Faster iteration matters more than another one-shot quality bump. https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/

8/ U.S. agencies accused DeepSeek, Moonshot, Alibaba and others of model distillation “at an industrial scale.” China rejected the charge. Synthetic training data is becoming a national-security and provenance issue. https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-accuses-chinese-ai-firms-industrial-scale-theft-ai-technology-2026-09-08/

9/ OpenAI says Codex has 25M users, an 80% Luna price cut drove ~10x usage, and Korean enterprise adoption grew ~28x YoY. It is pushing vertical systems and joint next-gen chip work with Samsung. https://www.reuters.com/world/china/openai-offers-ai-chip-design-touts-cost-advantage-over-open-source-cfo-says-2026-09-09/

10/ New analysis estimates 2019–2025 data improvements delivered 12x pretraining efficiency versus 3.7x from model recipes. It’s small-scale and assumption-heavy—but a sharp prompt to invest more in data quality. https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/pretraining-progress-is-mostly-data
